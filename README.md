# Vrinda_Store_Data_Analysis

A comprehensive data analysis project examining Vrinda Store's 2022 annual sales performance to identify customer behavior patterns and growth opportunities for 2023.

## 📊 Project Overview

This project analyzes a complete year of e-commerce sales data from Vrinda Store to understand customer purchasing patterns, identify top-performing segments, and provide actionable business insights for strategic decision-making.

**Objective:** Create an annual sales report to understand customers and grow sales in 2023.

## 📁 Dataset Information

- **Total Records:** 31,047 orders
- **Time Period:** January 2022 - December 2022
- **Total Revenue:** ₹21,176,377 (₹2.12 Crores)
- **Average Order Value:** ₹682
- **Geographic Coverage:** 50 states across India

### Data Fields

| Category | Fields |
|----------|--------|
| **Order Information** | Order ID, Date, Month, Status, Amount, Currency, Quantity |
| **Customer Data** | Customer ID, Gender, Age, Age Group |
| **Product Details** | SKU, Category, Size |
| **Sales Channel** | Channel, B2B indicator |
| **Shipping Info** | Ship City, Ship State, Ship Postal Code, Ship Country |

## 🎯 Analysis Questions Addressed

1. ✅ Compare sales and orders trends throughout the year
2. ✅ Identify the month with highest sales and order volume
3. ✅ Analyze purchasing behavior by gender (Men vs Women)
4. ✅ Examine different order statuses and their distribution
5. ✅ Identify top 10 states contributing to sales
6. ✅ Understand relationship between age and gender based on order volume
7. ✅ Determine which sales channels contribute maximum revenue
8. ✅ Identify the highest selling product categories

## 🔍 Key Findings

### 1. Monthly Sales Performance
- **Peak Month:** March 2022 with ₹19.28 lakhs (2,819 orders)
- **Lowest Month:** November 2022 with ₹16.15 lakhs (2,383 orders)
- **Trend:** Strong Q1 performance with gradual decline through the year

### 2. Customer Demographics

**Gender Distribution:**
- Women: 64% of sales (₹1.36 Crores)
- Men: 36% of sales (₹76.14 Lakhs)
- Women customers contribute 1.8x more revenue

**Age & Gender Breakdown:**
| Age Group | Men | Women | Key Insight |
|-----------|-----|-------|-------------|
| Adult (30-50) | 15.5% | **34.6%** | Largest segment |
| Teenage (18-29) | 9.2% | 21.1% | Growing segment |
| Senior (50+) | 5.9% | 13.7% | Loyal customers |

### 3. Order Status Distribution
- ✅ Delivered: 28,641 orders (92.3%)
- 🔄 Returned: 1,045 orders (3.4%)
- ❌ Cancelled: 844 orders (2.7%)
- 💰 Refunded: 517 orders (1.7%)

**92.3% delivery success rate indicates strong operational efficiency**

### 4. Top 5 Performing States

| Rank | State | Sales Amount |
|------|-------|--------------|
| 1 | Maharashtra | ₹29,90,221 |
| 2 | Karnataka | ₹26,46,358 |
| 3 | Uttar Pradesh | ₹21,04,659 |
| 4 | Telangana | ₹17,12,439 |
| 5 | Tamil Nadu | ₹16,78,877 |

*Top 5 states contribute 55% of total revenue*

### 5. Sales Channel Performance

| Channel | % of Orders | Rank |
|---------|-------------|------|
| Amazon | 35.5% | 🥇 |
| Myntra | 23.4% | 🥈 |
| Flipkart | 21.6% | 🥉 |
| Ajio | 6.2% | 4 |

*Top 3 channels drive 80.5% of all orders*

### 6. Product Categories
**Categories Available:** Kurta, Set, Western Dress, Top, Saree, Blouse, Ethnic Dress, Bottom

## 💡 Strategic Recommendations

### 1. Target Primary Customer Segment
**Action:** Focus marketing on adult women (30-50 years) who represent 34.6% of orders.
- Create targeted campaigns for this demographic
- Develop products specifically designed for this age group
- Concentrate efforts in top-performing states

### 2. Strengthen Channel Partnerships
**Action:** Deepen partnerships with Amazon, Myntra, and Flipkart (80% of business).
- Negotiate better product placement
- Plan exclusive launches on these platforms
- Invest in promotional opportunities

### 3. Geographic Expansion
**Action:** While maintaining strong presence in top 5 states, explore tier-2 and tier-3 cities.
- Regional marketing campaigns
- Improved logistics in emerging markets
- State-specific product offerings

### 4. Address Seasonal Decline
**Action:** Implement strategies to maintain consistent sales throughout the year.
- Strategic promotions during slower months (Sep-Dec)
- Festive campaign planning
- New product launches in Q3-Q4

### 5. Reduce Returns & Cancellations
**Action:** Minimize 7.8% revenue leakage from returns and cancellations.
- Improve product descriptions and images
- Enhanced sizing guides
- Better quality control measures

### 6. Expand Men's Category
**Action:** Grow the underdeveloped men's segment (36% of current sales).
- Develop dedicated men's product lines
- Targeted marketing campaigns for male customers
- Competitive pricing strategies

## 📈 Expected Impact

Implementing these recommendations could result in:
- 📊 15-20% increase in overall sales for 2023
- 📅 Improved revenue stability across all quarters
- 🎯 Higher customer lifetime value
- 👥 Expanded customer base through targeted growth
